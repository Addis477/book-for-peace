The Beginning

It started with a simple observation: everyone looks at the same sky. Whether in Addis Ababa or Tokyo, Nairobi or London, the sky connects us all. But we rarely see it as a bridge.

In 2026, Peace for You Network was born from this truth — not as another social platform, but as a living space where civil voices from every country could share one word: their peace. No algorithms, no noise — just the sky, and the people beneath it.

“One civil voice from every country”

195 nations. 8 billion stories. One network of peace.

The Sky Philosophy

The sky asks nothing of us. It simply exists — beautiful, changing, eternal. Peace for You Network is built on this same principle: we don't demand anything. We invite. We witness. And in that witnessing, we become the peace we seek.

Mr. Addis Abebayehu envisioned a place where anyone could look up — literally and metaphorically — and see themselves connected to everyone else. “The sky is the original peace treaty,” he says. “We just forgot to sign it.”
